Barnabas Journal

Privacy Policy — Last updated July 28, 2026

Overview

Barnabas Journal ("the app") is a personal encouragement and journaling app. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your journal never leaves your device, and we don't collect it.

Information We Collect

We collect none of your journal content. Everything you write or choose in the app — reflections, Barnabas moments, mood selections, favorites, streaks, and settings — is stored only in local storage on your own device (on mobile, via the device's app storage; on web, via your browser's local storage). We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to this information. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you if it's lost.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-up is required, and we don't ask for your name, email, or any other identifying information.
  • No analytics or usage tracking is built into the app.
  • No advertising, and no ad networks are integrated.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party, because none is collected in the first place.

Permissions the App Uses

  • Notifications — used only for the optional morning and evening reminders you can turn on or off yourself in Settings. These are scheduled entirely on your device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else to make them work.
  • Text-to-speech (Listen buttons) — uses your device's or browser's own built-in speech engine to read content aloud. No audio or text is sent anywhere external to do this.
  • File access (Backup/Restore) — used only when you tap "Export Backup" or "Restore Backup" yourself, to save or load a plain JSON file of your own data to your own device. This never happens automatically or in the background.

Automatic Device Backups

Android devices may automatically back up app data to the device owner's own Google account as part of the operating system's standard backup feature. This is a service provided and governed by Google, not by us — we have no access to those backups. You can control this in your device's own system settings.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since we do not collect journal content from any user at all.

Data Security and Your Control

Because your data stays on your device, its security is tied to your device's own security (screen lock, encryption, etc.), which we'd encourage you to keep enabled. You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app, or clear individual entries from within the app itself. Exported backup files are yours to keep, move, or delete as you see fit.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date above.

# Inbox triage

Use the email connector (Gmail or Outlook). Look at unread mail and anything
from the last 48 hours. Delegate to the `inbox-triage` subagent when there are
more than ~30 emails, so the main chat stays short.

Sort every email into one bucket:

- **Needs you**: a real person or organisation waiting on me, a deadline,
  school, health, money owed, bookings, anything from "People who always matter".
- **Can wait**: worth reading later, no action today.
- **Bin**: marketing, newsletters I never open, promotions, obvious spam.
- **Suspicious**: possible phishing or scam. Explain why in a few words.

Show "Needs you" first, one line each, with a suggested action
(reply / pay / book / read / ignore).

## Clearing out the bin

1. List the Bin emails as `sender — subject`, numbered.
2. Ask: "Bin all of these? Reply yes, or give me numbers to keep."
3. Only after I say yes, move them to Trash (never permanent delete).
4. Offer to unsubscribe from repeat senders, again only on my yes.

Never bin anything in "Needs you", "Suspicious" (I may want to report it), or
from people in my "always matter" list.

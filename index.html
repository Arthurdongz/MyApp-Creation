Welcome to Barnabas Journal

In the book of Acts, a man named Joseph was given a new name by the apostles: Barnabas — "son of encouragement." He had a gift for lifting others up with a kind word or a well-timed act of generosity.

This journal gives you a new verse, a word of encouragement, and a small "Barnabas moment" to do for someone else — one day at a time. A new day unlocks every day; you can always look back on days you've already lived, but not skip ahead.

Show up, be kind, and reflect a little. Stars, streaks, and badges are just there to notice how far you've come.
